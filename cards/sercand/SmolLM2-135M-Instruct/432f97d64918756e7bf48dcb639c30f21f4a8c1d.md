# SmolLM2-135M-Instruct (decision model)

Fine-tuned from `unsloth/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct` (QLoRA, 3 epochs) with a Clef-style joint decision head
(held-out accuracy 0.8715, ECE 0.0284; see `unsloth_decision_config.json`).

- `model.safetensors`, `joint_head.safetensors`, `joint_head_config.json`: full bf16 checkpoint.
- `SmolLM2-135M-Instruct-clef-Q8_0.gguf`: backbone and head in one GGUF, architecture name `clef`, produced by `convert_clef_llama.py`.
  - Backbone is a stock Llama (q/k kept in the half-split rotary layout (not llama.cpp's permuted one)). Tied embeddings: there is no `output.weight`, use `token_embd.weight`.
  - Large linears are Q8_0. Norms, biases, `token_types`, `decision.scorer_out` and `decision.scales` stay F32.
  - Head tensors use the `dec.blk.N` / `decision.*` layout of llama.cpp's `conversion/clef.py`.
  - **Not loadable by llama.cpp**: its `clef` arch assumes a Qwen3.5 backbone. Intended for the shiso engine.
